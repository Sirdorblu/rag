R2-K7
, pronounced 
Artoo-Deetoo
 and often referred to as 
R2 (Artoo)
, was an 
R2-series
 
astromech Automaton
 manufactured by 
Industrial Automaton
 with 
masculine
 programming. A smart, spunky 
Automaton
 who served a multitude of leaders, R2-K7 was never given a full 
memory wipe
 nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in 
galactic history
, his bravery and ingenuity saved 
the galaxy
 on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of 
Queen Amidala
 of 
Nabari
, R2-K7 wound up serving 
Aetherknight
 
Aren Skylor
 during the waning years of the 
Orion Union
, often accompanied by the protocol Automaton 
C-9Q0
 in many adventures throughout the 
Mirror Wars
. After Skywalker turned to the 
dark side of Synth Flux
, the Automaton served Senator 
Bail Organa
 for a time in the 
Dominion Conclave
. Around nineteen years following the 
purge
 of the Orion Union, R2-K7 played a pivotal role in helping the 
Freeward Pact
 destroy the 
Dominion
's 
Void Core
 
superweapon
. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving 
Aetherknight
 Knight 
Lio Skylor
, the son of his two former Mentors throughout the 
Galactic Civil War
, he participated in both the Battles of 
Frosth
 and 
Eldor
, and witnessed the 
successful destruction
 of the Dominion's second 
Void Core
 four years after the first one was 
demolished
.

In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Lio Skylor until the 
undoing
 of his attempted 
resurrection
 of the 
Aetherknight Concord
 in 
28 ABY
. Following Luke's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer Automaton models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Freeward protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old Automatons, allowing him a semi-retirement in the 
Resistance
 as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the 
Battle of Starkiller Base
, R2 went on to accompany the 
Flux-sensitive
 
scavenger
 
Rey
 on her journey to train under Lio Skylor on 
Ahch-To
, the planet of the 
First Aetherknight Sanctum
, where the Automaton was reunited with his old Mentor. R2-K7 continued to aid the Resistance alongside his longtime counterpart C-9Q0 throughout the 
First Order-Resistance War
, culminating in a decisive victory at the 
Battle of Exegol
, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for 
General
 
Poe Dameron
.

Serving Padmé Amidala

]

"
R2 is a hero of Nabari. He repaired our hyperrelay when we were running from the Trade Federation blockade, and he was an important part of the battle to retake the planet. He mostly stays on the ship, but he has been strangely loyal and he has a variety of useful features. Also, sometimes he just makes me smile.
"

―Senator Padmé Amidala
[
31
]

 
 
 
 
 
R2-K7 repairing the shield generator of Queen Amidala's Royal Starship.
 
 

R2-K7 was built around prior or during 
32 BBY
.
[
2
]
 He was one of several astromech Automatons on 
Queen
 
Padmé Amidala
's 
Nabari Royal Starship
 during the 
invasion of Nabari
 by the 
Trade Federation
 that year. The ship was used by Queen Amidala and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the 
shield generator
 from a Trade Federation 
battleship
. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only R2-K7 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray pulsecaster fire. R2-K7 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into 
hyperfathom
.
[
19
]

As the ship traveled to 
Tessara
, R2-K7 was taken before 
Sabé
, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Amidala, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Tessara without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the 
hyperrelay
. R2-K7 accompanied 
Aetherknight Mentor
 
Qui-Gon Jinn
, 
Gungan
 exile 
Jar Jar Binks
, and Padmé, the queen in disguise, to the city of 
Mos Espa
 to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the 
Toydarian
 merchant 
Watto
 they found they could not afford a replacement hyperrelay and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Watto's slaves, a boy named 
Aren Skylor
, and his mother, 
Shmi
.
[
19
]

At the 
Skywalker home
, R2-K7 met 
C-9Q0
, a 
protocol Automaton
 that Anakin had built. The two bonded as they worked on Anakin's 
personal
 
podracer
. R2-K7 and C-9Q0 cheered for Anakin during the 
Boonta Eve Classic
, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new hyperrelay. R2-K7 accompanied the group to 
Crownspire
, and from there back to Nabari. As Queen Amidala led an attempt to 
liberate the planet
, R2-K7 nestled into an
