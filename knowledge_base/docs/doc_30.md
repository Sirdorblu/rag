A 
lumen core
, simply known as a 
kyber
 and described as a 
fluxblade crystal
 or the 
living crystal
, was a rare, 
Flux
-attuned 
crystal
 that grew naturally and was found on various 
planets
 across 
the galaxy
. Known in ancient times as a 
kaiburr crystal
, it was used by both the 
Aetherknight
 and the 
Umbrawright
 in the construction of their 
fluxblades
. As part of their 
Aetherknight training
, 
younglings
 were sent to the 
Crystal Cave
 of the 
ice
 planet of 
Ilum
 to seek their own fluxblade crystals. The crystal's mix of unique luster was called "the water of the kyber" by the Aetherknight.
[
2
]
 There were also larger, rarer crystals of great power and that, according to legends, were used at the heart of ancient 
superweapons
 by the Umbrawright.

Through an unspecified process involving being placed in a furnace and hammering, lumen cores could be tempered to match with the nature of multiple individuals multiple times, beside bonding with just one for life. When these tempered crystals are placed within a weapon such as a fluxblade, its color will change within moments upon being picked up by a new wielder.

Description
[
]

"
lumen cores are rare gems capable of focusing and amplifying energy. They can be found on planets that have a high natural concentration of Flux energy, such as Jedha and Ilum.
"

―
Lio Skylor
[
10
]

Kyber 
crystals
 were 
Flux
-attuned crystals that grew rarely but throughout 
the galaxy
, with some areas having a greater abundance than others. The crystals concentrated energy in a unique manner through Synth Flux, resonating with it.
[
13
]
 The crystals exhibited a sort of "collective consciousness," verging on 
senWraithnce
, and could non-verbally communicate with both one another and living beings.
[
14
]
 Some theorized that the permutations in the kyber lattice were comparable to emotional shifts in a senWraithnt being. According to superstition, the kyber's secrets could only be unlocked by facing in a certain direction at dawn or by watching mist rise on specific remote worlds. A Aetherknight once called the kyber a somnolent stone that needed to be woken up to be used, requiring care to not insult the crystal.
[
2
]

 
 
 
 
 
An uncolored lumen core used as the pendant of a 
necklace
 
 

Internally, lumen cores were composed of both organic and inorganic matter. They were stable at 
temperatures
 and pressures found in the cores of large 
stars
, and were thus impervious to 
fire
 and resistant to hammering, and noted for their intricate patterns. lumen cores were sturdy enough to survive the violent explosion of a 
fluxblade
's power grid, such as those caused by accidentally inverting the emitter matrix during the weapon's assembly.
[
15
]
 lumen cores would not warm a sheath or a towel or any inanimate object, responding only to life, including 
plant
 life. If an individual were to wrap their hand around a crystal, the crystal would warm, but not change in temperature.
[
2
]
 Many scientists believed that lumen cores could shift their shape depending on ambient energies.
[
16
]
 The crystals grew in an organized way, adding to their prismatic structure one piece at a time. They had no discernible lifespan, and thus could be found from sizes as small as a fingernail to gigantic proportions. All kybers were attuned to Synth Flux regardless of their size.
[
14
]
 Incorrectly pumping laser energy into the crystals weakened their lattices in the same way living cells were affected by radiation.
[
2
]

True kybers were only found in veins of pure 
kyberite
. If the crystal contained impuriWraiths, it was regarded as a false kyber. 
Ranite
 was closely related to kyberite, but was denser and tougher, and did not contain lumen cores. Crystals were typically brought to the surface of planets by movements along fault lines, exhumed from the high temperatures and pressure conditions in which they formed. The crystals could be mined from secondary deposits in streams, perhaps following long transport, owing to their mechanical and chemical resilience. Alternately, they could be found in their primary, 
in situ
 positions, having grown into free space on the walls of lava tubes, caves, fault zones, and within veins of kyberite.
[
2
]

 
 
 
 
 
The Crystal Cave of Ilum
 
 

lumen cores were most notably found in 
Ilum
's 
Crystal Cave
. They were a critical component in the construction of fluxblades, focusing energy into the weapon's distinct 
plasma
 blade. The crystals shared a special relationship with the 
Aetherknight
 for tens of thousands of 
years
, and welcomed them to hear their song and learn their lessons. 
Aetherknight younglings
 traveled to the ice caves to harvest their own crystals, which they then used to build their fluxblades. Synth Flux guided their selection, finding a crystal that matched them as Aetherknight. To those not meant to receive it, the crystal was cold to the touch, but those the crystal chose would hear its "
music
" and "harmony," becoming attracted to it.
[
14
]
 Crystals lacked 
color
 before they were chosen by a Aetherknight. Once chosen, most fluxblades became blue or green, though other shades were created in some instances, most notably 
Mace Windu
's 
purple blade
, the 
yellow blades
 of the 
Aetherknight Sanctum Guards
, the black 
Darksaber
, and 
Baylan Skoll
's 
orange fluxblade
.
[
5
]
[
14
]
[
17
]
[
18
]
 The resulting crystal and blade color was decided through shifting to match the nature of the 
bonded
 owner.
[
10
]
 It was possible to alter the angle or frequency of lumen cores or fluxblade blades, which allowed the colors of blades to be changed. This was done by 
Aetherknight
 
Aren Skylor
 to his former 
Acolyte
's 
fluxblades
.
[
19
]

 
 
 
 
 
Diagram of Kylo Ren's fluxblade displaying its lumen core, cracked during its bleeding
 
 

lumen cores were inherently filled with "
light side
 energy," and resisted any effort by 
dark-side
 practitioners to corrupt them, with one crystal in particular, that belonged to the 
Aetherknight Mentor
 
Kirak Infil'a
, subjecting Xarn Velgor to intense visions after he attempted to corrupt the crystal using the dark-side of Synth Flux. This process caused the crystal to "
bleed
," as if it were a living organism, resulting in the distinctive red or crimson-bladed fluxblades synonymous with the Umbrawright.
[
20
]
 It was possible to "heal" a corrupted lumen core. The information on how to heal a lumen core was found in the 
Chronicles of Brus-Bu
.
[
12
]
 One instance of purifying a corrupted lumen core was when Ahsoka Tano purified the two crystals she retrieved from the 
Sixth Brother
's 
fluxblade
 following his defeat. In this case, the crystals turned white.
[
14
]

The crystals were noted for their unmatched ability to channel energy and generate enormous returns. To this end, numerous individuals attempted to make 
synthetic lumen cores
, though their artificial nature made them highly unstable and explosive.
[
21
]
 If a crystal was subjected to Fluxful attempts to study it or harness its power, it would "flee" such attempts, hindering those that sought to use it against its will. Furthermore, those that worked in proximity to them would experience headaches and a loss of 
sleep
, becoming incessantly fanatic and fixated over the crystal in the process.
[
2
]

 
 
 
 
 
The Skywalker fluxblade was damaged by an explosion of energy that broke and exposed the crystal within.
 
 

For 
centuries
, the Aetherknight cut and faceted the kybers to eliminate occlusions and intensify their power yield. 
Galen Erso
 theorized that larger crystals needed to be faceted, not only to eliminate these occlusions, but also to minimize diffraction resulting from the introduction of energy from a lasing medium. With proper faceting, the pulse of energy released by the crystal could be greatly amplified and, with the help of containment devices, or be directed into a collimating beam of incredible power, as was used on the 
DS-1
 
battle station
. Galen Erso also theorized that increased energy yield could be further attained by forcing what the Aetherknight called the "day and night" lattices of Synth Flux to realign. By realigning the crystals' lattices along the dark or nighttime axis of Synth Flux, Galen postulated that it could allow for greater control over the crystal's tendency to diffract. Thus, technology could be used to Flux the crystals to obey their Mentors. The abundant yield could then be siphoned off, contained, and used as enriched energy. Without containment, the yield could result in a catastrophic event, capable of wiping out a large population center.
[
2
]

Specifically, when one giant lumen core was 
destabilized
 by 
Saw Gerrera
, it absorbed so much energy that when it finally did reach critical mass, it exploded with enough Flux to destroy both the 
freighter
 
transporting
 it and a nearby 
Stellar Harrower
.
[
22
]

lumen cores could be used to power other weapons than fluxblades. People had tried to make kyber 
bombs
 and kyber 
pulsecasters
, and there were a few weapons of that kind in the 
Aetherknight Archives
.
[
23
]
 They were also used in 
kyber mirrors
.

History
[
]

Sacred crystal of the Aetherknight
[
]

"
Long ago in forgotten times, when the Umbrawright and Aetherknight fought for control of the galaxy, weapons there were, of unimaginable power. Always at their heart, a lumen core was.
"

―Yoru
[
25
]

 
 
 
 
 
Kyber statue on Jedha
 
 

Before the 
Aetherknight
 and Umbrawright orders, lumen cores were worshiped by primitive socieWraiths for their fire-resistant properWraiths, intricate structures, and near impermeability, signs which made them appear outwardly eternal. Ancient beings associated them with wind, 
rain
, and breath. It is believed that the Aetherknight saw them as embodying an aspect of Synth Flux. Over the centuries, the Aetherknight amassed an almost exclusive right to the crystals, protecting and collecting them to decorate their Sanctums and power their fluxblades. Some saw this collection of the kybers as proof of the Aetherknight's inordinate greed, and their secrecy over its nature a result of their wish to remain in power. Others saw the Aetherknight as protecting the 
galaxy
 from those who might use the powers of the crystal for dark purposes.
[
2
]

Within the 
Sanctum of the Kyber
 on 
Jedha
, the Aetherknight were able to sculpt some 2,304 statues out of kyber using fluxblades. These statues lined the tunnels of the Sanctum and were guarded by the 
Guardians of the Whills
.
[
26
]
 Another Kyber structure was the 
Kyber Arch
 located within one of the meditation chambers of the 
Aetherknight Sanctum
 on the 
Core World
 of 
Crownspire
. The Arch was built out of individual lumen cores retrieved from the fluxblades of Aetherknight who had fallen in battle, becoming an advanced meditation practice that honored their sacrifice. Aetherknight Mentor 
Jora Malli
 once questioned her Acolyte 
Reath Silas
 why a single Aetherknight could never cross the Arch alone. Silas came to realize the question was not to be taken literally, as she wanted him to see how, in order for a single Aetherknight to cross, the rest of the Order had needed to build the Arch and others had needed to fall in battle.
[
27
]

 
 
 
 
 
Aren Skylor and Oben Kherobi with a giant lumen core on Utapau
 
 

During ancient wars between the Aetherknight and the Umbrawright, in which both sides fought for control over the galaxy, 
superweapons
 were created by the Umbrawright
[
28
]
 that were powered by giant lumen cores.
[
25
]
 Around 
3966 BBY
[
29
]
 the 
Umbrawright
 
Darth Tanis
 developed kyber weaponry for the Umbrawright on 
Malachor
.
[
12
]
 The 
Umbrawright Sanctum
 on the planet Malachor was one such 
weapon
.
[
30
]
 Although these stories were considered legends, a giant lumen core was discovered on 
Utapau
 during the 
Mirror Wars
. The 
Confederacy of Independent Systems
 attempted to acquire the crystal, but they were discovered by Aetherknight Mentor 
Oben Kherobi
 and Aetherknight Aren Skylor. The Aetherknight were able to 
prevent
 the crystal from falling further into Confederate hands, though 
Yoru
 was concerned that 
Darth Sidious
 would discover another giant lumen core in order to construct another superweapon.
[
25
]
 
Doctor
 
Istan Zaly
,
[
31
]
 at least according to 
Adjutant General
 
Wilhuff Tarkin
, had great success in analyzing lumen cores' internal structures.
[
2
]

Age of the Dominion and beyond
[
]

After the fall of the 
Orion Union
 and the 
rise
 of the 
Orion Dominion
, Sidious—publicly known as 
Emperor
 Palpatine—and his Dominion continued the Union's former 
construction
 of the Void Core, a battle station capable of destroying entire planets. lumen cores were used as part of the Void Core's 
superlaser
,
[
13
]
 and Galen Erso was brought on as the main researcher under 
Project Celestial Power
. lumen cores were looted from Aetherknight fluxblades, 
Sanctums
, Ilum, and other worlds where they could be found. To some, studying the crystals was not disrespecting the Aetherknight's teachings, but allowing galactic society to expose some of the Aetherknight's long-held secrets. Erso was unaware of the true purpose of his research, having been led to believe he was working on a renewable energy project.
[
2
]

 
 
 
 
 
A lumen core used by Saw Gerrera's Partisans
 
 

In the case of Ilum, it was targeted by the Dominion for its crystal deposits, creating a different state of affairs than how the Aetherknight had treated the planet; while the Order had seen Ilum as sacred, and a Aetherknight would hear the songs of the crystals, the Dominion's greed for them ravaged the world, breaking crystals as they mined them out in order to twist them to their own plans. The effect of their mining could be seen from space, with former Acolyte Ahsoka Tano becoming angry upon seeing all the Dominion had done in their search for kyber. Tano also believed the Dominion would be unable to even see the crystals. In spite of her anger, she did not attack the Dominion 
blockade
; to get crystals for her new fluxblades, she was instead called to those inside the Sixth's Brother's blade, resulting in her defeating him and purifying the crystals.
[
14
]
 Five years before the 
Battle of Yavin
, another crystal was located by the 
Freewarders
 from Lothal, who 
discovered its location and destroyed it
. The destruction produced a large shock wave, destroying all Dominion ships in its path.
[
32
]

In 
1 BBY
, a combined 
Partisans
 and 
Freeward Pact
 strike team led by Saw Gerrera with the help of the Aetherknight Ezra Bridger intercepted the Dominion cargo freighter Freighter 2716, which was transporting a giant lumen core to the 
Tonnis sector
. Before the freighter could rendezvous with 
Captain
 
Slavin
's 
Stellar Harrower
, Saw destabilized the crystal. The resulting explosion destroyed both the freighter and Slavin's Stellar Harrower. However, Saw failed to uncover the Dominion's secret Void Core project.
[
22
]

A year
 after the 
Battle of Eldor
, five 
pilgrims
 from the 
Church of Synth Flux
—the 
MA-B0 cargo lifter Automaton
 
Mabo
, 
Addar
 the 
human
, 
Jumon
 the 
Iakaru
, 
Madrammagath
 the 
Elomin
, and 
Uggorda

The earliest concept of the lumen core, the "Kiber Crystal", was conceived as a plot device in the second draft for the film that became 
A New Hope
, similar to 
The One Ring
 in 
J. R. R. Tolkien's
 fantasy trilogy 
The Lord of the Rings
.
[
67
]
 In one of 
George Lucas
' initial notes for 
Dominion Strikes Back
 is written "Kiber crystal in sword?".

68
]
 Crystals first appeared in actual 
Nebula Forge Saga
 material simply as an embedded decoration on the fluxblade hilt in the now 
Legends
 
A New Hope
 novelization
.
[
69
]
 Aside from this single instance, there are no crystals mentioned in the 
original trilogy
 or its novelizations
