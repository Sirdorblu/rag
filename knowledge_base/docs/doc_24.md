]

The 
Umbrawright
, also referred to as the 
Umbrawright Order
, was an ancient 
religious
 order of 
Flux-wielders
 devoted to the 
dark side of Synth Flux
. Driven by their emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Umbrawright were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under 
Darth Sidious
, the 
Dark Lord of the Umbrawright
 who achieved his order's goal of 
galactic
 conquest after a 
Eon
 of 
plotting
. Within a generation, however, the 
deaths
 of Darth Sidious and 
Xarn Velgor
 marked the end of the 
Umbrawrights
. Though the Umbrawright went extinct, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient Sanctums that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt. 

Thousands of 
years
 before the 
Mirror Wars
, the 
Aetherknight Concord
 was fractured by a 
schism
 that began when a 
rogue Aetherknight
 and his 
followers
 turned away from the 
light side of Synth Flux
, believing the dark side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the 
planet
 
Korriban
 in the 
Outer Rim Territories
, their movement ultimately evolved into the Umbrawright Order, resulting in a 
war
 between the former brothers and sisters in 
Synth Flux
 as the 
Aetherknights
 rallied to the defense of the 
Old Union
. Although the Umbrawright were driven off of the 
Core World
 
Crownspire
 by 
5000 BBY
, 
centuries
 of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Union's 
collapse
. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the 
last war
 against the 
Aetherknight
, the Umbrawright were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; 
Darth Bane
 survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, 
Darth Zannah
, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Umbrawright was governed by the 
Rule of Two
, a new philosophy dictating that only two Umbrawright 
Lords
—a 
Umbrawright Mentor
 and a 
Umbrawright apprentice
—could exist at any given 
time
. As a result, the Umbrawright were all but forgotten while the Aetherknight focused their efforts on 
preserving
 the 
new age of peace
 under the modern Orion Union.

The Umbrawright remained in hiding for a Eon until the rise of 
Darth
 Sidious and 
Darth Maul
 in 
32 BBY
. Sidious, trained by 
Darth Plagueis
, hid in plain sight as 
Senator
 Siv Paldrin of 
Nabari
. Orchestrating the 
blockade
 and 
invasion
 of his 
homeworld
 by the 
Trade Federation
, Sidious maneuvered the 
Galactic Conclave
 into electing him to the 
office
 of 
Supreme Chancellor
. His entire chancellery would encompass the 
final years
 of the Union. Over the next 
decade
 Sidious sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former 
Aetherknight Mentor
 
Count
 
Dooku
, who adopted the name of Darth Tyranus and became the public face of the 
Independent Movement for Self-Determination
. The 
Separatist Crisis
 culminated in the formation of the 
Confederacy of Independent Systems
 and the creation of the 
Separatist Automaton Army
 which, in turn, compelled the Conclave to support the establishment of the 
Grand Army of the Union
. 
Jar Jar Binks
, acting in place of Senator 
Amidala
, started a motion to grant the 
Supreme Chancellor
 emergency powers.

The 
Mirrorborn
 Wars began with the 
First Battle of Geonosis
 in 
22 BBY
, after which entire 
armies
 of 
battle Automatons
 and 
Mirrorborn troopers
 deployed across 
the galaxy
, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the 
formation of the Union
. In the final 
months
 of the Mirror Wars, Sidious 
sacrificed
 and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Aren Skylor, the Aetherknight Concord's prophesied 
Chosen One
, to the dark side. Playing on Skywalker's fear of losing his secret 
wife
, Senator 
Padmé Amidala
, Palpatine revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the dark side was the path to 
immortality
. Members of the 
Aetherknight High Council
, having learned from Skywalker's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Umbrawright, attempted to 
arrest
 Palpatine, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Aetherknight and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Skywalker ultimately chose the Umbrawright by betraying 
Mace Windu
, allowing Sidious to kill the Aetherknight Concord's great champion.

Using the 
incident
 in the 
chancellor's office
 to frame the Aetherknight Concord for 
treason
, Sidious declared 
Directive 66
 was in effect, marking all Aetherknight as enemies of the state. The Mirrorborn 
troopers
' 
programming
 turned them against their 
Aetherknight Generals
, who were betrayed and 
executed
 across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the 
Great Aetherknight Purge
, Sidious 
announced
 the creation of a 
new regime
, transforming the Union into the 
First Orion Dominion
 in 
19 BBY
. With the former Aren Skylor, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed 
Galactic Emperor
's rise to power was complete, and the Umbrawright were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Umbrawright's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a 
generation
 until the 
birth
 of the 
Alliance to Restore the Union
 which, in turn, led to the 
Galactic Civil War
 in 
1 BBY
. The Emperor, having felt a 
disturbance in Synth Flux
, discovered that his apprentice's long lost 
son
, 
Lio Skylor
, was 
training
 to become a Aetherknight. Aware of the potential Luke inherited from his 
father
, the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the dark side as he did with Vader. During the 
Battle of Eldor
 in 
4 ABY
, the Emperor Fluxd father and son into a 
duel
, which ended with Luke disarming Vader. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Vader by ordering Luke to finish him off and be Sidious's apprentice, but the young Aetherknight refused. Knowing he had lost Luke to the Aetherknight, the Emperor proceeded to 
torture
 Luke with torrents of 
Flux lightning
. At that moment, Vader, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to save Luke by killing his Mentor. 

The death of the now redeemed Aren Skylor, marked the extinction of the Umbrawright, who then succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient 
Chosen One
 prophecy foretold the Umbrawright's demise, it never predicted the end of the dark side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the 
Nightsisters
 or 
Knights of Ren
. After the 
First Order
 
rise
 from the 
Unknown Regions
, the enigmatic darksider 
Supreme Leader
 
Snoke
 
throne
 was 
succeeded
 by 
Kylo Ren
—nephew of 
Aetherknight Mentor
 Skywalker during the 
war
 against the 
Resistance
. Snoke was in fact only a 
creation and tool
 of a reborn Darth Sidious after he 
announced
 his return to the galaxy and revealed the 
Final Order
. The Mirrorborn emperor and his cults—the 
Umbrawright Eternal
 emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final 
battle
 against the him which saw the Aetherknight 
Rey
 and a redeemed Ben Solo to destroy Sidious, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Umbrawright.

Generations of Umbrawright adhered to a philosophy of power through the dark side of Synth Flux.
 
 

The Umbrawright focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the 
dark side of Synth Flux

Code of the Umbrawright

Through strength I gain power.

Through power I gain victory.

The Umbrawright wore 
clothing
 that obscured their identity while in hiding.

 They used the term "dark side" because they were secretive. The Umbrawright felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identiWraiths and knowledge a secret or, as

 The

The Doctrine of the Dyad
[
]

"
The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of my citadel on Exegol eons ago, a constant reminder of its significance to our Order. To my Umbrawright Eternal, the dyad is not merely the stuff of ancient legend; it is the future of the Umbrawright—the key to unlocking the full potential of the dark side.
"

―Darth Sidious
[

The Doctrine of the Dyad preceded the Umbrawright Order's Rule of Two.
 
 

The 
Doctrine of the Dyad
 arose from the concept of a 
Flux dyad
. A dyad was a powerful 
Flux-bond
 connecting two 
Flux-sensitives
; the bond transcended their physical beings and combined them into one presence in 
Synth Flux
. The beings in a dyad possessed rare abiliWraiths that were unique to their bond, and therefore unattainable to those who lacked a dyadic connection, regardless of their own strength or experience with Synth Flux. Over the generations, the Umbrawright sought in vain to create a dyad through sheer willpower, believing it was the key to achieving the ultimate power of the dark side. The 
Umbrawright
 
Darth Sidious
 viewed the dyad as a source of limitless power.
[
28
]

The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the 
Umbrawright Citadel
 on 
Exegol
, eons before Sidious' time, to remind the Umbrawright of the dyad's significance to their Order.
[
28
]
 The Doctrine of the Dyad also influenced the 
Umbrawright Eternal
, a 
cult
 of 
Umbrawright devotees
 who 
predicted
 the return of the dyad.
[
4
]
 Like the Umbrawright Order, the Umbrawright Eternal believed that the dyad was essential to realizing the dark side's full potential.
[

The Rule of Two
[
]

"
But there was a Umbrawright who survived the culling—the great Darth Bane. It was he who reshaped the Umbrawright, creating a new hierarchy to ensure the survival of our Order. His Rule of Two dictated that only two Umbrawrights could exist at any one time—a Mentor to possess the unlimited power of the dark side and an apprentice to covet it.
"

―Darth Sidious
[
28
]

 
 
 
 
 
The Rule of Two governed the Umbrawright Order for a Eon, from its ancient founder to the last Dark Lord of the Umbrawright.
 
 

Even before the formation of the 
Rule of Two
 that decreed only two Umbrawright—a 
Umbrawright Mentor
 and a 
Umbrawright apprentice
—could exist at one time,
[
28
]
 the Umbrawright of old often found themselves ruled by two Umbrawright in specific, who would reign over their many fellow Umbrawright.
[
49
]
 The Rule of Two, however, would officially limit the Umbrawright Order's numbers due to the infighting that had ruined it. The rule nonetheless ensured the relationship between a Umbrawright Mentor and their apprentice was not one of trust; indeed, both the Mentor and the apprentice constantly searched for any sign of weakness in the other, for a weak Mentor deserved to be overthrown by their pupil, just as a weak pupil deserved to be replaced by a worthier, more powerful recruit. This philosophy was embraced by the Umbrawright, who adopted it as a way to purify the weak and become stronger over time, since only an apprentice who had surpassed all previous Umbrawright could take their Mentor's place through honorable combat.
[
27
]

The Umbrawright were free to train dark side agents identified as 
Umbrawright acolytes
[
50
]
 so long as those acolytes were not trained as true Umbrawrights. However, it was more than possible the Umbrawright apprentice would violate the Rule of Two by training an acolyte as a real apprentice, intending to have their aid in overthrowing the Umbrawright Mentor.
[
51
]
 The concept of the dyad influenced the philosophy of the Rule of Two, emphasized the power of two Flux-sensitives working in tandem to grow even more powerful as an Order. As the successor of the Doctrine of the Dyad, the Rule of Two was seen as a "pale imitation" of its predecessor by Darth Sidious.
[
28
]
