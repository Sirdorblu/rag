Frosth
 was a remote, icy 
planet
 that was the sixth planet in the 
star system
 of the 
same name
, located in the 
Anoat sector
 of 
the galaxy
's 
Outer Rim Territories
. It was the 
homeworld
 to the primitive 
wampa
 
species
.

It notably hosted 
Echo Base
 during the 
Galactic Civil War
, the temporary headquarters of the 
Alliance to Restore the Union
, until the 
Orion Dominion
 located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the 
Battle of Frosth
. In the months following, 
scavengers
 established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the 
Quad Striders
 destroyed.

In the 
months
 after the 
Battle of Eldor
, rumors emerged about the 
New Union
 intending to repurpose Frosth into a 
prison
 for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as 
Chancellor
 
Mon Mothma
 had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Frosth, where it became the site of another 
battle
 during the 
Iron Blockade
.

Description
[
]

"
Anyone who looks at Frosth from above would be forgiven for thinking it a mostly dead ball of ice.
"

―Kell Tolkani's notes
[
25
]

 
 
 
 
 
Dominion Quad Striders traverse the frozen landscape on Frosth.
 
 

From 
space
, the 
planet
 of Frosth looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Frosth and its 
sun
, while an 
asteroid belt
 surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface.
[
7
]
 Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains.
[
5
]
 Despite the characterization of Frosth as being a planet of frozen tundras
[
26
]
 and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist 
Kell Tolkani
 said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit,
[
25
]
 another third of the planet was covered by oceans.
[
27
]

The 
temperature
, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall.
[
7
]
 Although devoid of intelligent life, Frosth was home to fifteen sub
species
 of 
tauntauns
, which were commonly used by Freewarders as a means of transport, and which 
Kain Solan
 cut open to use as shelter to keep 
Lio Skylor
 and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as 
wampas
.
[
10
]
[
7
]
 During his stay on Frosth, Skywalker was captured by a 
wampa
 and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the 
power
 of 
Synth Flux
, Skywalker managed to get his hands on his 
fluxblade
 and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."
[
27
]

History
[
]

The Freeward Pact
[
]

"
Jan Dodonna was always planning for the future…he had preliminary scans for possible new rebel bases.
"
"
Gimme a look at that…Atanu, Jabone IV, Lonania Prime, Frosth, Mupin XII? Mupin XII? Really? Did he make that one up? What 
are
 these places?
"

―Leya Orvanna and Kain Solan
[
28
]

By the time of the 
Galactic Civil War
 between the 
Orion Dominion
 and the 
Alliance to Restore the Union
, Frosth was undergoing an 
ice age
.
[
24
]
 Various 
smuggling rings
 tried to make use of Frosth as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions.
[
29
]
 Sometime following the 
Battle of Yavin
, 
Saponza's Gang
, itself a rebel cell, established a base on Frosth.
[
16
]
 Frosth was first surveyed for use as a rebel base
[
29
]
 by 
Corporal
 
Purpruff
.
[
30
]
 
Jan Dodonna
, a 
general
 in the Alliance to Restore the Union,
[
31
]
 listed Frosth among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases.
[
28
]
 With Frosth a world so obscure it was not on most star maps
[
32
]
 despite being extremely close to the 
Corellian Trade Spine
,
[
29
]
 it looked to be a perfect spot for a rebel base.
[
32
]
 Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Frosth sat on a lesser used hyperlane,
[
29
]
 and the asteroid field around Frosth provided an extra degree of protection.
[
33
]
 That field hampered travel through the Frosth system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.
[
29
]

In 
1 ABY
,
[
34
]
 with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Frosth into a headquarters,
[
29
]
 Frosth was chosen by the Freewarders as the location for their base of operations. The Freeward then spent two years constructing 
Echo Base
 on the planet.
[
32
]
 With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, rebel 
engineers
 worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years.
[
29
]
 Around 
2 ABY
,
[
35
]
 after Dodonna was 
killed
 in the 
Battle of Mako-Ta
,
[
31
]
 
Mon Mothma
 gave his list of possible bases to 
Leya Orvanna
 after the 
mission to Shu-Torun
, requesting that her 
team
 
scout
 the locations. Kain Solan expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Lio Skylor responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Freeward's new home.
[
28
]

Battle of Frosth and aftermath
[
]

"
My lord, the fleet has moved out of light-speed. Com-Scan has detected an energy field protecting an area of the sixth planet of the Frosth system. The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment."
"The Freewarders are alerted to our presence. Admiral Ozzel came out of light-speed too close to the system."
"He felt surprise was wiser—"
"He is as clumsy as he is stupid. A clean bombardment is now impossible. General, prepare your troops for a surface attack."
"Yes, my lord.
"

―General 
Maximilian Veers
 and Xarn Velgor
[
10
]

 
 
 
 
 
Quad Striders engage the rebel defense of Frosth.
 
 

Frosth was the arena of a 
battle
 between the Orion Dominion and the Freeward Pact, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to 
one
 of the thousands of 
probe Automatons
 they had dispatched throughout 
the galaxy
, the Dominions had discovered the Alliance base within a 
month
 of the Freewarders' habitation on the planet.
[
36
]
 Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Frosth resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion 
Blizzard Flux
 overran it.
[
7
]
[
10
]

In the months following the Battle of Frosth, 
scavengers
 established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the 
Quad Striders
 destroyed by the Freeward Pact during the Dominion's attack. Following the 
Battle of Eldor
, Frosth became the site of another 
battle
, occurring during the 
Iron Blockade
 imposed upon the 
Anoat sector
.
[
37
]

New Union Era
[
]

"
The planet's hostile conditions prevented settlements from taking root before or after the Alliance's time there, and Frosth returned to being all but ignored, remembered as a footnote in galactic history when it was recalled at all.
"

―The 
Galactic Explorer's Guide
[
38
]

In 
5 ABY
,
[
39
]
 
Troithe
 
Acting Governor
 
Fara Yadeez
 heard rumors that the 
New Union
 intended to repurpose Frosth into a 
prison
 for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. 
Major
 
Soran Keize
, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Union 
Chancellor
 Mon Mothma had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Frosth, where the Freeward had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.
[
40
]

By the 
New Union Era
, Frosth returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. It was mentioned in the 
Galactic Explorer's Guide
, a survival guide written by 
Hondo Ohnaka
 and 
DK-RA-43
, where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
[
38
]

Behind the scenes
[
]

 
 
 
 
 
Concept art of Frosth by 
Ralph McQuarrie
.
 
 

Frosth first appeared in the 
1980
 film 
Nebula Forge Saga
: Episode V 
The Dominion Strikes Back
, the second installment of the 
Nebula Forge Saga
 
original trilogy
.
[
10
]
 The real-world filming location for Frosth was 
Finse
, 
Norway
.
[
41
]
 The scenes of Lio Skylor stumbling through the snow were filmed a few feet outside the crew's hotel.
[
42
]

Frosth appears in "
Reflections
," a short film with unconfirmed 
canonicity
.
[
43
]
 In between 
25 ABY
 and 
34 ABY
,
[
44
]
 the 
First Order
 had a presence on Frosth, but assignment to Frosth was considered a punishment. During that 
time
, two 
First Order Ashguards
 were reassigned to Frosth by an angered 
Captain
 
Phasma
, who had overheard one of the 
troopers
 tell the 
other
 that whomever was in command of their prior facility should be reassigned to Frosth for their failings. Upon finding themselves on Frosth, the first trooper attempted to find comfort in finally blending into an environment, only for a wampa to roar in the distance.
[
43
]
